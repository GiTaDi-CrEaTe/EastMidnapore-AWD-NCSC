Perched Water Table & Root Zone Hydration Dynamics

In lowland clay-loam soil with an impermeable plough pan at -22 cm, water does not drain freely into deep regional aquifers. Instead, it forms a perched water table. This model simulates capillary rise into the 0 to -15 cm root zone, demonstrating why soil remains adequately hydrated down to the -15 cm threshold without inducing plant water deficit.

Practical Field Directives for Farmers

  • Pipe Installation: Install pipe in an average elevation area of the plot 15 days after transplanting once roots are anchored.
  • Irrigation Trigger: Reflood with 5 cm standing water only when the water inside the tube drops to the bottom of the perforated section (-15 cm).
  • Flowering Period Safety Rule: During panicle emergence and flowering (Days 58 to 72), maintain shallow standing water (2 to 3 cm) at all times to prevent spikelet sterility.
  • Pre-Harvest Drainage: Terminate all irrigation 10 to 12 days prior to harvest to facilitate sickle harvesting and grain dry-down.

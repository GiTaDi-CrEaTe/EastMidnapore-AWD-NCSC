Key Field Recommendations for Purba Medinipur Farmers

  • Pani Pipe Placement: Install one Pani Pipe per bigha in an average field spot (avoid depressions and bund edges).
  • Reflood Schedule: Do not irrigate until water drops to the -15 cm bottom mark. Reflood to 5 cm.
  • CRITICAL FLOWERING WARNING: During panicle emergence & flowering (Days 58 to 72), maintain shallow standing water (2–3 cm) constantly to avoid grain sterility!
  • Terminal Ripening Drainage: Stop all irrigation 10 days before harvest to hasten uniform grain ripening.
